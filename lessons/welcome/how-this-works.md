# How this works

This site is your **library**. The teaching itself happens in conversation with Claude, and each lesson gets written up here so you can review it anywhere, any time.

## The loop

1. You pick a topic and we start with a short diagnostic.
2. We work through it together, with you doing the thinking.
3. The lesson is saved here, with questions to test yourself later.
4. You come back and review, because memory fades fast without it.

> Reading something feels like learning. Being able to explain it without looking is actual learning.

## Check yourself

Tap a question to reveal the answer, but **try to answer it first**. That effort is what makes it stick.

?? Why is it better to try answering before revealing the answer? || Pulling an answer out of memory strengthens it far more than re-reading, even when your attempt is wrong.

?? What should you do if a lesson loses you? || Say so. Being stuck is useful information, and the lesson can be re-taught a different way.

## Tips

- Review old lessons after a few days, not just right after learning them.
- Explain a lesson out loud in your own words.
- Mark a lesson complete only when you could teach it.
